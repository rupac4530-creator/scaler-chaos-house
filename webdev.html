🛠️ THE 4 STEPS OF BUILDING A WEB PAGE

What Web Dev Sir asks vs what actually happens

Step 1: HTML

🗿

"Structure bana do."

Simple <div>, <h1>, <p>. Everything is peaceful and organized. You feel like a senior developer.

Step 2: CSS

🔥

"Ab beautiful bana do."

Adding gradients, hover effects, geometric patterns. You spend 45 minutes trying to center a button, but it looks gorgeous!

Step 3: JavaScript

💀

"Now make it work."

Uncaught TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined. You add event listeners that trigger 500 infinite loops.

Step 4: Me

😭

"Wait... why is everything broken?"

You fixed one bug in CSS and now the navbar is upside down and the footer has migrated to the top of the screen.
